Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceBahamas ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information about you when you use voicebahamas.com and the Earn Tings panellist platform we operate.

This policy applies to all Bahamian residents who visit our website, create an account, or take part in surveys through VoiceBahamas and Earn Tings.

1. Who we are

VoiceBahamas is a market research platform serving The Bahamas. It is operated by BahaNext Group Ltd (currently in formation), hereafter referred to as "the operator". The panel and reward infrastructure are provided by Earn Tings.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at hello@voicebahamas.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide when signing up

  • First name
  • Mobile phone number (used to verify your identity via SMS one-time code)
  • Age bracket
  • Gender
  • Island of residence
  • General location (verified at signup, see section 2.3)

2.2 Information you provide when completing the optional profile survey

  • Household composition, employment status, education, and income range
  • Housing type and constituency
  • Vehicle, internet, mobile, and banking provider preferences
  • Shopping, media, and lifestyle preferences

Completing the profile survey is optional but unlocks more relevant survey invitations.

2.3 Location verification

We use location data for fraud prevention and to confirm responses originate from The Bahamas.

2.4 Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system, and browser
  • IP address (used for fraud prevention and rough geographic verification)
  • Referral source (e.g. campaign link clicked, referring friend)
  • Survey responses, points balance, redemption activity

3. How we use your information

We use the information collected to:

  • Verify you are an eligible Bahamian resident
  • Match you to relevant survey opportunities
  • Track points earned and rewards owed
  • Deliver rewards (digital voucher codes are sent via SMS or in-app)
  • Prevent duplicate accounts and fraud
  • Improve the platform
  • Communicate important account or survey notifications

4. How we share information

We share insights derived from survey responses with research clients.

5. Service providers

We use third-party service providers (e.g. infrastructure, messaging, reward fulfilment) under appropriate safeguards.

6. Data retention

We retain data for as long as necessary for our operational, research, and compliance needs. On account deletion, we may anonymise or remove personal data.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your account by emailing us
  • Object to processing for direct marketing purposes

To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@voicebahamas.com.

8. Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect personal data.

9. Children

VoiceBahamas is open to Bahamian residents aged 18 and older.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and prevent fraud. We also use Google Analytics to understand site usage; this may set cookies in your browser. Google Analytics data does not include your name, phone, or email.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

VoiceBahamas
Email: hello@voicebahamas.com
Nassau, The Bahamas

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Bahamian law. If you believe your data rights have been violated, you may contact the relevant Bahamian data protection authority.
