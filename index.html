Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Who can join?

Anyone who is a Bahamian resident and at least 18 years old. We verify residency during signup.

Is this really free?

Yes. There is no cost to join VoiceBahamas, ever. We are paid by the companies who commission research, not by you.

How do rewards work?

Many surveys offer rewards for participation. The type of reward (digital voucher, gift card) and amount depends on the specific survey. Not all surveys carry rewards. Reward details are shown before you start any survey.

Who runs VoiceBahamas?

VoiceBahamas is operated from Nassau by a Bahamian-based team. Reward fulfilment and panel management are powered by Earn Tings. For details, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

How often will I be contacted?

Survey invitations vary depending on what research is active and whether you match the audience. Most active members are invited to a few surveys per month.

Can I leave at any time?

Yes. You can deactivate your account at any time from within the app. No questions asked.
